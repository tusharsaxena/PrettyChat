# 02 — Deviations (Ka0s Pretty Chat)

**Run date:** 2026-09-23
**Audited against:** Ka0s WoW Addon Standard **v2.64.0 (2026-09-23)**
**ID prefix:** `PC-` (stable across runs). New IDs continue from the 2026-09-08 run, which ended at
`PC-76`; this run's new IDs are `PC-77` … `PC-102`.
**Previous run:** `docs/audits/2026-09-08/` (against v2.39.0), frozen and untouched.

Sections are cited as `filename-§N`, or by bare filename for the eleven section files with no numbered
subsections. **Severity is impact, never rule strength** (`AUDIT.md` step 5); the **Level** column
names the rule's strength separately, so a Low that fails a MUST is visible as both.

## Tally, with its basis stated

- **Headline — root deviations only: 26.**
  - By grade: **High 1 · Medium 2 · Low 20 · Info 3.**
  - By level: **MUST 20 · SHOULD 2 · contract 1** (options-ui-§5's descriptive header contract, not
    an explicit MUST — PC-96) **· observation 3** (the Info rows fail no rule).
- **Total including `derived from` dependents: 30** — the 26 roots plus 4 dependents (PC-78, PC-79,
  PC-80 derived from PC-77; PC-91 derived from PC-90), all four Low and all four MUST.
  - By grade: **High 1 · Medium 2 · Low 24 · Info 3.**
  - MUST failures including dependents: **24** (20 roots + 4 dependents).
- **Recorded deviations (accepted, not counted): 8** ratified register rows whose triggers have not
  fired (table below). A ninth row, `debug-logging-§2`, is not accepted as a live deviation — it
  records behavior the standard treats as compliant and is filed for retirement (PC-88).

**The one High is graded by the playbook, not by reach.** `AUDIT.md`'s re-vendor check states that a
vendored tag with no bundle and no register row "is a **High** finding", and that specific rule is
followed here. No player, SavedVariables file or session can reach it; if the two grading rules in
`AUDIT.md` are read against each other it would be Low. It is recorded as High so the headline
matches what every other audit on this playbook files for the same gap.

**The two Mediums are the only findings a player can see.** The Categories page's **Defaults** button
resets one tab where the rule makes it page-wide (PC-81), and the General page's **Test** button
tooltip tells the player `/pc test` prints to chat when it has written to the debug console since
2026-09-16 (PC-87). Nothing is broken; both are a wrong answer on a path a player uses.

**Versus 2026-09-08: 7 → 26 roots.** Four of that run's seven closed (PC-71, PC-72, PC-74, PC-75);
three carry (PC-60, PC-69, PC-73) and PC-76 carries as a re-measured Info. Twenty-two are new: nine
arise from rules added between v2.40.0 and v2.64.0 (the re-vendor-bundle MUST, the generator home, the
event-registration `pcall` MUST, the carve-out's wording, the perf skip-reason MUST and others); the
rest are drift introduced since the last run or were missed by it (the TOC denominator, the Defaults
scope, compat, savedvariables-§2, the bus threshold).
