Nothing in this suite pinned what the re-cut moved: no case asserts a library title-bar control
without a tooltip, an attached tooltip below its control, a mark's `icon.hot` hover, the
Diagnostics link's orange rest, a skin's `close.rest` or `list.header.bg`. No test changed.

## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

Ka0s Pretty Chat draws **no window of its own** with a title bar or header strip. It is frameless:
the one `CreateFrame` in its source is the hidden combat watcher in `modules/Override.lua`. The
debug console, its copy window and the minimap menu are the library's, and they take the new
tooltips and colors from the re-copy alone. The settings panel's header (its Defaults and Test
buttons) is drawn by the library's options composer. `NS.MakeCloseButton` still has no caller in
the addon. The step was skipped: no test was added and no `W.HeaderButtons` adoption was needed.
