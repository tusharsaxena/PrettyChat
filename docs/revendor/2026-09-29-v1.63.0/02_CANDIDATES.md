# Candidates (PrettyChat)

One surface arrived, and it has one candidate use here:

- **The `profile` verb** (Slash minor 17: the `profiles` descriptor field, `Sl:CliProfile`,
  `Sl:ProfileSwitch`). PrettyChat has user-visible profiles since SP-PC-01 (the Profiles page), and no
  verb reaches them from chat.

`lib.ProfileNames` has no separate use: PrettyChat has no profile sub-tree of its own to list from.
